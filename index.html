Good to know

What area do you serve?

La Crosse, Wisconsin. Call or text and we will confirm your address.

Do you use a plow or a snowblower?

We use a plow or a snowblower depending on your property. We will tell you which when we give your estimate.

When does a Season Contract visit happen?

Service begins automatically once snow accumulation reaches at least 3 inches, when it is safe for the crews to service. The season runs from November 1 to March 31. The terms explain the details.

Is salt included?

Salt is included unless you prefer otherwise. For one time visits, salt is available on request.

What about pavers, sprinklers or other hazards?

Tell us about anything hidden or delicate, such as pavers, sprinkler heads or pet fences, and we will work around it.

How do I book a one time visit?

Schedule a time on our Picktime booking page. When you schedule with Picktime, we will be able to do your order.
