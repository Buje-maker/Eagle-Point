About Eagle Point

Eagle Point Outdoor Services began as a college snow removal business. It aims to provide winter employment for college students and donates 5% of proceeds to the Catholic Charities Warming Center.

Questions

What area do you serve?

La Crosse, Wisconsin. Call or text and we will confirm your address.

When does a Season Contract visit happen?

Service starts automatically once snow reaches your selected height, when it is safe for the crew. The season runs Nov 1 to Mar 31.

Do you use a plow or a snowblower?

We use a plow or a snowblower depending on your property. We will tell you which when we give your quote.

Is salt included?

Salt is included unless you prefer otherwise. For per push service, salt is available on request.

What about pavers, sprinklers or other hazards?

Tell us about anything hidden or delicate and we will work around it.

How do I request per push service?

Tap "Request per push service" on this page. Tell us your size and a day that works, and we will text you to confirm your price and time. Please ask at least 8 hours before the snow starts.

How do I get a price?

Every property is different, so we give a free quote. Call or text (507) 494-8960.
