No time for shoveling?

Early mornings, long days and late evenings leave little room for a driveway full of snow. We clear it so you can get on with your day.

Tired of the back strain?

Heavy snow is hard on the back and knees. Let someone else handle the heavy lifting, and keep your steps and walkways clear and safe.

Two ways to hire us

One time visit

Need the snow cleared once? We schedule a time that works for you. No commitment. Salt is available on request.

Season Contract

10 visits with salt included. Service begins automatically once snow accumulation reaches at least 3 inches, with priority over one time customers.

How it works

  1. Get an estimate

    Call or text for a free estimate. We confirm your size and any notes about your property.

  2. Choose how to hire us

    Choose a one time visit or a Season Contract. We send your price and the next steps.

  3. We clear the snow

    One time customers are cleared at the booked time. Contract customers are cleared automatically, when it is safe to do so and weather permits.
