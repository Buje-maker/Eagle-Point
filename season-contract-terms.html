Season Contract Terms

These terms go with your Season Contract quote. If anything is unclear, call or text (507) 494-8960 and we will go over it with you.

  1. The season

    Your Season Contract runs November 1 to March 31 and includes the number of visits shown on your quote. Salt is included unless you prefer otherwise.

  2. Accepting your quote

    Paying your invoice accepts your quote and these terms. No signature is needed. After you pay, reply to our welcome message so we have your details on file.

  3. When we come

    We come once uncleared snow reaches the inch height on your quote. Back to back snowfalls combine. At the end of our route we pass by again to make sure your driveway is clear.

  4. One visit per day

    We make one visit every 24 hours. If it snows again after 24 hours, we return.

  5. Big storms

    A storm of 18 inches or more, or clearing during the storm, counts as 2 visits, never more than 2.

  6. Drifting and blocked vehicles

    If drifting or a blocked vehicle needs a return visit within 24 hours, it is billed at 50% of our on demand rate.

  7. The city plow ridge

    The ridge the city plow leaves at the end of your drive and at curb ramps is a special request. We do not charge for it and it does not count as a visit.

  8. Unused visits

    Up to 2 unused visits carry over if you renew within one month, to a maximum of 12 visits. We let you know when you reach your 8th visit and 2 remain.

  9. Hazards and special requests

    Tell us about anything hidden or delicate, such as pavers, sprinkler heads, pet fences or steep spots, and we will work around it.

  10. Referrals

    When a neighbor buys a Season Contract because of you, you earn one free visit.

  11. Refunds

    Refunds are available before Nov 1st.
