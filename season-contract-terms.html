Season Contract Terms

These terms go with your Season Contract quote. If anything is unclear, call or text (507) 494-8960 and we will go over it with you.

  1. The season

    Your Season Contract runs November 1 to March 31 and includes the number of visits shown on your quote. Salt is included unless you prefer otherwise.

  2. Accepting your quote

    Paying your invoice accepts your quote and these terms. No signature is needed. After you pay, reply to our welcome message so we have your details on file.

  3. Dispatch

    We normally come out at 3 inches of snow. Your quote may list a different depth. We aim to clear your driveway before 7:30 AM. After the storm is completely over, we aim to return within 1 to 2 hours to finish.

  4. Visits

    Back to back snowfalls count as 1 visit. Snowfalls that each reach the depth on your quote and happen 2 or more hours apart count as separate visits.

  5. Heavy snow

    If 3 inches or more is expected around 7:30 AM, we aim to clear an initial path by 7:30 AM and return to finish after the heavy snow has stopped. The initial path and the return count as 1 visit. A snowfall of 10 inches or more counts as 2 visits.

  6. Sidewalks and front walks

    We aim to clear sidewalks and front walks as part of the same visit as your driveway. Timing may vary with conditions.

  7. City Plowing

    At the end of our route, we go back and clear the curb side of your driveway if the city plow has pushed snow back in. This is included and does not count as a separate visit.

  8. Visits beyond your quote

    Your Season Contract is paid upfront. Visits beyond the number on your quote are billed monthly at the per visit rate on your quote, based on the actual number of visits that month.

  9. Unused visits

    Up to 2 unused visits carry over if you renew within one month, to a maximum of 12 visits. We let you know when you reach your 8th visit and 2 remain.

  10. Special Requests

    Tell us about anything hidden or delicate, such as pavers, sprinkler heads, pet fences or steep spots, and we will work around it.

  11. Referrals

    When a neighbor buys a Season Contract because of you, you earn one free visit.

  12. Refunds

    Refunds are available before Nov 1st.

Questions about your quote or these terms? Call or text (507) 494-8960 or email Eaglepointoutdoor@gmail.com.
