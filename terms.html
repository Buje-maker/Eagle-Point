SERVICE TERMS

Eagle Point Outdoor Services

Last updated November 24, 2026

These Service Terms apply to snow removal and groundcare services provided by Eagle Point Outdoor Services (“Eagle Point”) to the customer named on the quote or proposal (“Client”). If a signed quote or proposal states different terms, the signed document governs where it differs.

Scope

Eagle Point shall furnish all labor, equipment and materials required to perform the services in the areas listed in the quote or proposal. Areas are estimates and are verified at a site visit before work begins.

Standard of work

Eagle Point shall perform the services in a timely and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry practice.

Acceptance and service period

The Client accepts a quote or proposal by signing it, by written confirmation by email or text, or by paying an invoice. Service begins on acceptance and runs through the end date stated in the quote or proposal. Unless the quote or proposal states otherwise, the winter season runs November 1 through April 30.

Pricing models

The quote or proposal states one of the following pricing models.

  • Per push. The Client pays the price per push stated in the quote or proposal for each push.
  • Per inch. The Client pays for each push at the rate for the depth section that contains the storm total, as stated in the quote or proposal. The depth sections are:
    • 1.5 to 3 inches
    • Over 3 to 6 inches
    • Over 6 to 10 inches
    • Over 10 to 14 inches
    Storms over 14 inches are priced as stated in the quote or proposal. The storm total is measured as stated under Snow removal.
  • Seasonal. The Client pays a set price, before the first visit, for the number of visits and the season dates stated in the quote. Visits beyond that number are billed monthly at the per visit rate in the quote. The Season Contract terms also apply.

Snow removal

  • Service is dispatched automatically when the storm total reaches the trigger depth stated in the quote or proposal, without a call from the Client.
  • The trigger depth is the storm total required to start an automatic dispatch. It is not the interval that defines the per push rate. As an example, 3 inches of snowfall does not constitute two pushes.
  • The cost per push is defined as completely clearing all snow from the entire area listed in the quote or proposal.
  • Snow that continues to fall, or returns within 2 hours, is one push. Snowfalls that occur before Eagle Point has made a main pass are also combined into one push. A new snowfall that occurs 2 or more hours after the main pass and reaches the trigger depth is a separate push.
  • Eagle Point returns for one cleanup pass after the snow has stopped. The cleanup pass is included in the push and is not charged separately.
  • Snowfall totals are measured on site by Eagle Point, and that measurement governs. Totals may be verified against the total inches reported by NOAA for the La Crosse area.

Time and schedule

Arrival times are estimates and depend on the weather, safety and the Eagle Point route. Any arrival window stated in a quote or proposal applies.

Weather and safety

Eagle Point may delay or limit service when conditions are unsafe, access is blocked, or equipment fails, and shall resume as soon as it is safe. A delay under these conditions is not a breach of these terms. Eagle Point does not charge for a push it did not complete. Eagle Point shall make reasonable efforts to notify the Client of a delay by text or call.

Events beyond our control

Neither party is in breach for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as severe weather, road closures, illness, equipment failure, supply shortages or orders of civil authority. The affected party shall notify the other and resume as soon as it can.

Access and safety

The Client shall keep the areas reasonably accessible and shall move vehicles and equipment before a storm when possible. Eagle Point cannot clear under or around vehicles that block the work area. Eagle Point shall use caution around pedestrians and children, and the Client shall keep people out of the work area during service.

Obstacles

The Client shall point out all obstacles hidden under the snow, such as pavers, sprinkler heads, pet fences and uneven spots. Eagle Point may place markers at them and removes the markers at the end of the season. Eagle Point is not responsible for unmarked obstacles.

Snow piles

Snow shall be placed on the Client’s property where it does not obstruct entrances, sight lines or parking, and shall not be placed on the roadway or public right of way. Snow relocation or hauling is not included and is quoted separately.

Salting

Salt is applied when the quote or proposal includes it or the Client requests it, at the rates stated in the quote or proposal. Salt is spread evenly and kept off grass and landscape areas. Eagle Point removes salt it applied in excess at no cost to the Client.

Damage and liability

  • Snow removal can cause minor wear to edges, pavers, gravel and surfaces that were already cracked, loose or damaged.
  • Eagle Point is not responsible for damage to gravel or unpaved surfaces, turf, items hidden under the snow or not made known to Eagle Point, pre existing damage, or vehicles left in the areas.
  • The Client shall report any claimed damage within 48 hours with a photo. Eagle Point shall repair or compensate for damage caused through its negligence.
  • Salt melts snow and ice but cannot make a surface fully ice free, and it can affect some surfaces, plants and pets. Salt does not guarantee that a surface is safe from slipping.
  • Eagle Point is not responsible for injury from slipping on a surface after service, since snow and ice can reform. The Client remains responsible for keeping the property safe for visitors.

Payment

Invoices are submitted on the first day of each month for the previous month’s service. Payment is due within 7 days of the invoice date. Failure to pay on time means Eagle Point may choose to suspend service until the account is paid in full.

Invoices are sent electronically to the billing email address provided by the Client, or by mail to the billing address provided by the Client. Invoices list the date, location, snowfall and number of pushes. The Client shall notify Eagle Point in writing of any disputed item within 7 days of the invoice date. Undisputed amounts remain due on the due date.

Season Contracts are paid before the first visit and have their own terms.

Referrals

A neighbor is a different household at a different address. The neighbor must name the Client as the referrer, in their notes or in their reply before paying.

  • If the Client has a Season Contract and the neighbor purchases a Season Contract, one additional visit is added to the Client’s Season Contract after the neighbor’s payment is received.
  • If the Client is a per push customer and the neighbor becomes a paying customer, a credit equal to one push at the lowest rate in the Client’s quote is applied to the Client’s next invoice after the neighbor’s first payment is received.

The additional visit or credit has no cash value and cannot be transferred. If the neighbor receives a refund, it is removed.

Default

If a party fails to perform a term, the other party may give written notice describing the default. If it is not fixed within 14 days, the other party may end the agreement. This does not limit Eagle Point’s right to suspend service for non payment.

Termination

Either party may end service by giving 14 days written notice. The Client pays for all pushes completed through the end of the notice period. Eagle Point may choose to suspend service immediately for non payment.

Privacy

Eagle Point uses the Client’s contact information only to provide service, send visit updates and handle payment, and does not sell or share it.

Disputes

The parties shall try to resolve any dispute by direct discussion for 30 days before starting any court action. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Wisconsin. Any dispute will be handled in La Crosse County.

Notices

Written notice may be given by email, text or mail to the contact details on the quote or proposal.

General

These terms and the accepted quote or proposal are the entire agreement between the parties and replace any earlier agreement about the same services. Changes must be in writing and confirmed by text or email by both sides. The failure of either party to enforce a term is not a waiver of the right to enforce it later. Neither party may transfer this agreement without the other’s written consent. Eagle Point may use employees or subcontractors and remains responsible for the service. If any part of these terms cannot be enforced, the rest stays in force.
